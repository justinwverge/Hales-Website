Signs you need HVAC repair

  • Warm air blowing from the vents in cooling mode
  • Cold air from the vents in heating mode
  • System won't turn on, or shuts off too soon (short cycling)
  • Grinding, squealing, buzzing or banging noises
  • Water around the indoor unit or a clogged drain line
  • Ice on the refrigerant lines or outdoor coil
  • Energy bills creeping up for no reason
  • Some rooms much hotter or colder than others
  • A burning or musty smell when the system runs
  • Thermostat not responding or reading wrong

What we repair

We work on central air conditioners, heat pumps, gas furnaces, air handlers and ductless systems. Common repairs include:

  • Capacitors, contactors and relays
  • Condenser fan and blower motors
  • Refrigerant leak detection and repair
  • Condensate drains, pumps and float switches
  • Thermostats and control boards
  • Heat pump defrost and reversing valve issues
  • Furnace igniters, flame sensors and gas valves
  • Electrical wiring, breakers and disconnects

How our repair visits work

Our technicians use modern diagnostic tools to test the whole system — electrical, airflow and refrigerant — instead of guessing and swapping parts. Once we know what's wrong, we explain it in plain English, show you what we found and give you your options with pricing. Nothing happens until you say go.

We use high-quality parts so the repair lasts. And if your system is old enough that a repair doesn't make financial sense, we'll tell you honestly — and give you the numbers on both repair and replacement so you can decide.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you come out for a repair?

Book online to see our available appointment times, or call 706-765-7219. We're open seven days a week, 7am to 7pm, and work to get no-cool and no-heat calls back up and running as fast as possible.

Do you repair brands other than Carrier?

Yes. We repair most major brands of air conditioners, heat pumps and furnaces.

Should I repair or replace my AC?

If your system is under 10–12 years old and the repair is reasonable, repairing usually makes sense. If it's older, uses R-22 refrigerant, or is breaking down often, replacement may save you money. We'll give you honest numbers on both.

Why is my AC running but not cooling?

Common causes are a dirty filter or coil, a failed capacitor or fan motor, low refrigerant from a leak, or a frozen coil. Turn the system off if you see ice and schedule a diagnostic visit.
