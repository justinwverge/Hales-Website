FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What areas does Hale's Heating & Cooling serve?

We're based at 1120 Mars Hill Road in Watkinsville and serve Watkinsville, Athens, Bogart, Bishop, Statham, Winder, Monroe, Winterville and the surrounding Oconee, Clarke, Barrow and Walton County areas.

How do I schedule service?

Click any Schedule Now button to book online through our FieldEdge scheduler and pick a time that works for you, or call us at 706-765-7219.

Do you work on brands other than Carrier?

Yes. We're a Carrier Factory Authorized Dealer, but our NATE-certified technicians repair and maintain most major brands of air conditioners, heat pumps and furnaces.

Do you offer financing on new systems?

Yes. We offer financing through Wells Fargo on qualifying Carrier systems, with monthly payment options, subject to credit approval.

Should I repair or replace my HVAC system?

It depends on the age of the system, the cost of the repair, how often it's breaking down and how efficient it is. We'll lay out both options honestly, with prices, and let you decide.

Do you service commercial buildings?

Yes. We provide repair, maintenance and replacement for light commercial properties including offices, retail and storage facilities.

Need heating or AC service?

Book online in under a minute, or call and talk to a real person in our Watkinsville office.
